Frequently Asked Questions

What is a shoji floor lamp?

A standing lamp with a paper or fabric shade set in a wooden frame, inspired by traditional Japanese shoji screens. The shade softens the light so a room feels warm and calm instead of brightly lit.

What bulb should I use in a shoji lamp?

A warm white LED bulb, usually around 2700K, keeps the glow soft and close to candlelight. Cool white bulbs tend to look harsh behind a paper shade.

Are shoji floor lamps bright enough to read by?

Most are designed for ambient light rather than task lighting. If you plan to read, place a smaller reading lamp nearby or choose a model that accepts a higher lumen bulb.

Do shoji lamps work in small apartments?

Yes. Their slim, vertical shape takes up very little floor space, which makes them a good fit for corners in small living rooms and bedrooms.

Is the shade made of real washi paper?

It varies by model. Some use real washi paper, while many use a paper-look fabric or plastic panel that is easier to clean. Check the product listing for the exact material.
