Measure before you choose

Nightstand dimensions: height, width & depth

Choose a bedside table by its measurements, not just its finish. Use the size reference below, measure your mattress and room, then check a candidate with the calculator. The same method applies to a Japandi nightstand or any other style.

A useful shopping reference is 23–28 inches high, 21–28 inches wide and 16–20 inches deep. These are Wayfair’s standard-size ranges, not a universal furniture standard. How wide should a nightstand be?

Measure the free space on each side of the bed separately. Subtract the bed-side and wall-side gaps you want to keep, then compare the remainder with the table’s full width, including projecting feet or handles. A queen or king mattress label alone cannot tell you that available width. Try a taped outline with your lamp, phone and other essentials before choosing.

How deep should a nightstand be?

Measure from the planned back edge to the front edge, allowing for the baseboard and cables behind it. Mark the full footprint on the floor and try your walking route. A 17-inch-deep cabinet does not tell you how far its door or drawer extends; obtain that separate measurement when space is tight.

Measure four things before shopping

  1. Mattress-top height (H): measure vertically from the floor to the top of the mattress, including any topper you use. Do not use the bed frame’s height or a pillow as the reference.
  2. Usable bedside width (S): measure from the outermost edge of the bed frame to the nearest wall or obstruction. Subtract any gaps you want to leave on both sides. Measure each side of the bed separately.
  3. Usable depth: tape the proposed footprint onto the floor. Keep your actual walking route, closet access, outlets and curtains usable; a table fitting into the width does not prove there is room in front.
  4. Opening space: find the drawer’s full extension or door’s swing envelope. Overall table depth does not tell you either of these. If the maker does not specify them, ask before buying when clearance is tight.

Test the reach: with a helper or stable existing surface, try the proposed tabletop height while lying and sitting in bed. Check whether you can reach the lamp switch and put down a glass comfortably. Our calculator reports dimensions; it cannot assess your reach or accessibility needs.

A worked example: a 26-inch mattress top

A 22-inch table is 4 inches lower: 22 − 26 = −4. With 24 inches beside the frame and 4 inches reserved for both gaps together, the body-width allowance is 20 inches. A 19-inch-wide table leaves 1 inch within that allowance. Neither result proves that a door will open or the height will feel comfortable.

Common sizing questions

What are standard nightstand dimensions?

See the sourced dimension table for height, width and depth in inches and centimeters. Retailer ranges differ; compare the exact model with your mattress and bedside space.

Will a 26-inch-high nightstand work beside my bed?

Compare 26 inches with your measured mattress-top height. It is level with a 26-inch mattress top, 2 inches above a 24-inch mattress top and 2 inches below a 28-inch mattress top. Those are height differences, not comfort guarantees. Try the reach and check width, depth and storage opening separately. None of the three audited products is listed as 26 inches high.

Should a nightstand be higher or lower than the bed?

Compare the top of the nightstand with the top of the mattress, not the frame. Use a similar height as a reference, then try your own reach. We do not treat a fixed number of inches above or below as a universal comfort rule.

What size nightstand works with a king or queen bed?

The bed label alone cannot determine it. Mattress height, the frame’s outer width, space on each side, tabletop needs and opening clearance matter. Measure the real setup rather than choosing by a bed-size chart alone.

Does “natural oak” mean solid oak?

No. It can identify a color or finish. Look for a component-level material specification. Our checked Mina manufacturer page lists engineered wood and laminate among its materials; the other two listings also identify engineered wood.

Can I trust the width check if a drawer extension is unknown?

Only as body-width arithmetic. It does not establish operating clearance, usable walking space, assembly tolerance or a safe installation. Get the opening dimensions before buying for a tight space and follow the maker’s assembly and anchoring instructions.
