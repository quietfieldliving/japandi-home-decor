# Kitchen island stool spacing: evidence and draft plan

Status: research-only draft, not published. Do not merge before editorial QA.

Primary search intent: how many stools fit at a 6-foot or 8-foot kitchen island.

Source: National Kitchen and Bath Association, Kitchen Planning Guidelines (2022), guideline 9: recommended 24-inch width of knee space per seated diner, and depths of 18, 15, and 12 inches at table/counter/bar heights of 30, 36, and 42 inches respectively.

Proposed original utility: compare floor(usable seating length / 24) with floor(usable seating length / 30) for lengths of 48 to 120 inches. Label both as planning estimates, not guarantees. Check actual stool width, arms, end obstructions, knee space, and aisle clearance.
