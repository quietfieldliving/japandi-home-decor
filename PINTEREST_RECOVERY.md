# Pinterest Recovery Sprint — 2026-10

## Why this sprint exists

Quiet Field Living's Pinterest Organic distribution collapsed across multiple categories at the same time:

- 2026-09-24: 9,378 impressions
- 2026-09-25: 4,214
- 2026-09-26: 686
- 2026-09-27: 432
- 2026-09-28: 401
- 2026-09-29: 697
- 2026-09-30: 125

This is now treated as a distribution-recovery problem, not ordinary day-to-day fluctuation.

## Strategy

For 14 days, publish **2 genuinely fresh Pins per day**. Do not mass-upload near-duplicate images.

Rules:

1. Every Pin gets a new 1000x1500 (2:3) image.
2. No text baked into the image for this sprint.
3. Different composition, lighting, furniture shape, room context or crop for every creative.
4. Keep the destination URL on the existing category LP.
5. Do not alter the Priority Amazon CTA experiment while restoring Pinterest traffic.
6. Focus on proven volume categories plus high-CTR expansion categories.
7. Re-evaluate after 7 days and 14 days, not after every single Pin.
8. If a fresh Pin gets >0.50% outbound CTR with meaningful impressions, produce 2–3 new visual variants around that *intent*, not copies of the same image.

## Measurement

Track daily:

- Impressions
- Outbound clicks
- Outbound CTR
- Saves
- Engagements

Track by fresh Pin at 24h / 72h / 7d where available.

Recovery thresholds:

- **Weak:** profile remains below 1,000 impressions/day after 7 days of fresh publishing.
- **Partial recovery:** 1,000–5,000/day.
- **Functional recovery:** >5,000/day for 3 consecutive days.
- **Prior-range recovery:** >8,000/day for 3 consecutive days.

Do not call the strategy successful merely because one Pin spikes. The goal is restoration of repeatable account-level distribution and outbound traffic.

## Revenue objective

Target: **$1,000+/month by 2026-12-31**.

This target is not achievable by impression recovery alone. The working funnel remains:

Fresh Pinterest discovery → category LP → Amazon click → order / commission.

Therefore the Pinterest sprint restores top-of-funnel traffic while the existing Priority Amazon CTA experiment measures downstream conversion.
