Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Quiet Field Living collects only the information needed to provide requested downloads, operate the site, measure performance, and send optional email updates when a visitor chooses to receive them.

Information we collect

If you request the Japandi Room Setup Checklist, we collect the email address you submit. If you check the optional marketing box, we also store that preference. The site may collect standard analytics data such as page views, referral information, and outbound link clicks.

How we use it

Your email address is used to deliver the requested checklist. Optional marketing emails are sent only when you choose that option. Analytics data is used to understand which guides and links are useful.

Service providers

Email delivery and subscriber management are handled by Resend. Website hosting is provided through GitHub Pages, and server-side form processing is handled by Cloudflare Workers. These providers may process data as needed to operate those services.

Affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase after following one of them, Quiet Field Living may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Your choices

You can leave the marketing checkbox unchecked and still receive the requested checklist. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option. Unsubscribing does not prevent delivery of a file or message you specifically request later.

Data minimization

Quiet Field Living does not sell subscriber email addresses. Data is kept only as needed to operate the mailing list, provide requested content, measure performance, and meet reasonable record-keeping needs.
